# Study data provenance

Original installed Bible databases are retained. Per-book and dictionary assets derive from those databases.

## KJV supplied-word italics

[eBible KJV USFM](https://ebible.org/Scriptures/eng-kjv_usfm.zip), with its [public-domain notice](https://ebible.org/eng-kjv/copyright.htm). Parsed all 31,102 canonical verses. USFM add markers identify supplied words. Emphasis was transferred onto the installed wording: 13,736 verse records have supplied-word emphasis. Verified 1 John 2:23 explicitly. The sidecar preserves the installed wording.

## Missing Enoch addresses

[Charles 1917 PDF](https://cleartexteditions.com/ct/public%20domain/1%20enoch%20-%20translated%20by%20%20R.%20H.%20Charles.pdf). The [Sacred Text Archive title page](https://sacred-texts.com/bib/boe/boe000.htm) identifies the edition and public-domain status. The PDF was rendered and inspected. Only the 44 internally missing addresses are supplemented, and results label those as Charles supplements. Existing verses keep the existing vault translation.

Two omitted printed verse labels were checked against verse-addressed Charles text: [22:4](https://biblescribeapp.com/verse.php?b=68&v=52920) and [93:2](https://www.mannafest.faith/read/apocrypha/1-enoch/93/2). The transposed subverse labels in 5:6 are preserved. This is a gap supplement, not a new critical edition.

## Hebrew and dictionaries

The missing Hebrew display verses derive from the project's supplied STEPBible TAHOT CC BY files: 2,083 recovered verse records. Includes numbering differences such as Joel, Malachi, and Daniel. Every entry in the split dictionaries compares exactly with its original: 19,570 Strong's entries, 10,714 LXX entries, and 10,714 LSJ entries.

## Search

All searchable e/o/s/m fields and word order are preserved. Identical word records share storage. Transliterations remain in per-book study files. Tests compare every result of 18 queries against the actual installed original corpus. The Hebrew display repairs are separate from the unchanged search-corpus content.
