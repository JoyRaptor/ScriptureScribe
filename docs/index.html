✦ Why ScriptureScribe

A second brain
for your Bible.

Bible software like Logos and e-Sword was built around a library, with a notes pane added on. ScriptureScribe starts from your notes. Everything you write becomes part of how you read Scripture, and the Bible, the original languages and your own commentary all live in one place.

01 Hover

Hover a reference.
The verse appears.

ScriptureScribe spots references as you type, like Gen 1:1, Jn 3:16, 1 Cor 13:4-8 and 2Tim 3:16, and underlines them without adding anything to your markdown. Hover one to read the verse, switch translations, or follow its cross-references.

When you want the verse in the note for good, embed it as a link, a footnote or a quote block. Embed All converts every reference in the note in a single undo step.

  • Nothing added to your files
  • KJV built in, works offline
  • 3 embed styles
  • Live translation switcher

02 Read

The Bible,
lit up by your notes.

Open Bible mode beside your writing and read any chapter. Every verse that one of your notes cites is highlighted in that note's own color, and a verse cited by several notes blends their colors. Plain verses stay plain, so at a glance you see where your study has been and where it hasn't.

Click a highlighted verse and In your notes lists every note that cites it, with the sentence around each citation, one click from the note itself. That includes notes other people wrote, if they're in your vault.

  • A color per note
  • Click a verse, read the notes
  • All 66 books

03 Research

Every word,
down to the root.

Switch to Research mode for an interlinear view of every verse: the original word, its transliteration, Strong's number and grammar. Tap any word below to open its lexicon entry.

Strong's search

Type G5485 or H7225 to get the lexicon entry and every verse that uses that word. You can also search in English, Greek or Hebrew, or search by meaning.

Your notes + cross-references

In your notes lists every note in your vault that cites the passage. Below it, cross-references are listed for 28,752 verses.

05 Install

Up and running
in two minutes.

No coding and no terminal. The download already includes all the Bible, Hebrew, Greek and lexicon data.

  1. 1

    Download

    Get scripture-scribe.zip from the button below.

  2. 2

    Unzip

    You get one folder named scripture-scribe.

  3. 3

    Drop it in

    Move that folder into <your vault>/.obsidian/plugins/. The .obsidian folder is hidden: on Windows turn on View → Show → Hidden items, and on Mac press ⌘⇧.

  4. 4

    Switch it on

    Settings → Community plugins: turn off Restricted mode, click refresh, and enable ScriptureScribe.
